PREORDER FAQ

Do I pay full price upfront?

No. You pay a small advance (typically ৳500–৳1000) to secure your slot. The remainder is paid on delivery via COD or bKash.

What if delivery is delayed?

We'll notify you immediately of any delay with a new estimated date. You may cancel for a full refund anytime before shipping.

Can I customise a preorder jersey?

Yes! Name, number, patch and sleeve type customisation is available on select preorder jerseys at the time of ordering.

How do I track my preorder?

Log in to your account and visit "My Orders". Your preorder status is updated in real-time from Pending → Confirmed → Processing → Shipped → Delivered.
